# Next release (draft)

## Smoother to use
- **Pinned bar:** while the panel is below the view, Cards, Copy for AI, Make card
  and Save to Missed Qs stay at the bottom of the pane.
- **Make card opens beside the page**, so the explanation stays readable. Select text
  to add it to the card you're writing.
- **New popup:** Today, Study and Settings tabs, real switches, connection status at
  the top, and a first-question step in setup.
- **Faster to respond:** the panel reacts to the page at once instead of on a
  one-second timer, and holds its space while loading.
- **Rows start closed** on each question. A setting keeps them open if you prefer.
- **Results buttons** sit beside the page's own on Coursology, and a block with
  nothing missed says so instead of offering empty actions.
- Image overlays count their images, and the arrow keys step through them.
- "Opening in Anki…" confirms the D key and the Anki buttons.

## Copy for AI
- Five rewritten prompts, plus **Explain from Basics**. Links and the question bank's
  name are kept out of the copied text.

## Look and accessibility
- One type, spacing and colour system across panel, dialogs and popup, in light and dark.
- Green now means one thing: Save to Missed Qs.
- The panel follows the question bank's light or dark look; the theme setting is gone.
- Supports Reduce Motion, Increase Contrast and forced colours.
- Text and badges meet contrast and size minimums.

## Fixed
- An image in a reopened row could stay an empty box after going back to a question.
