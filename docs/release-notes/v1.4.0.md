A reliability and security release, from a full audit of 1.3.1 against the
real extension, the real add-on and a real Coursology account. **Update both
halves**: the extension (Chrome Web Store) and the Mnestic Bridge add-on
(AnkiWeb → Tools → Add-ons → Check for Updates). Either works with the other's
previous version; the fixes marked *add-on* need the new add-on.

## Fixed

- **Make a card → Create card did nothing** in 1.3.1. It creates the card again,
  and now asks before adding one you already have.
- **The panel showed the previous question's cards.** If Anki answered slowly
  and you moved on, the old question's cards and resources could land on the new
  one — and Save, Preview and the image keys then acted on the old one. Each
  question is now built on its own, and a late answer for another question is
  thrown away.
- **The panel vanished** when Coursology re-drew the explanation (toggling its
  question list). It comes straight back.
- **"Couldn't reach Anki" was a dead end** until you changed question. It now
  says *why* — Anki closed, not paired, busy, or an add-on update needed — retries
  on its own, and recovers the moment you paste a pairing code.
- **The results toolbar appeared where it shouldn't**, such as your Previous
  tests list, where it could send your test scores to Anki as question ids. It
  now appears on results pages only.
- **Keyboard shortcuts were dead with an Arabic (or any non-Latin) keyboard
  layout.** They now follow the physical key. Typing in a text box inside a
  web component no longer triggers them, and holding a key no longer flickers
  the overlay.
- **"Study them in Anki" left out questions saved with "No subdeck".**

## Seeing the whole card

- **Extra and Additional Resources have their own rows** at the top of the
  panel — text and images, card by card — and **E / A show the whole field**
  over the question, not just its images. On Step 2 and 3 cards these fields
  are most of the card, and a text-only Extra used to answer "No Extra image".
- **Preview shows the whole card**: every filled field. Text, Extra,
  Additional Resources and your Missed Questions notes are open; First Aid,
  Sketchy, Physeo and the rest are one click away and load their images only
  when opened, so it stays fast.
- **Save to Missed Qs can show the full selected card** (collapsed until you
  open it), so you can choose the right card without leaving the dialog.

## Saving to Missed Qs, and undoing it

- A save now **unsuspends only the card you chose** — not every card of every
  note on the question.
- **Undo reverses exactly that save:** it re-suspends the cards the save
  unsuspended, puts a moved card back in its own deck, and deletes a copy it
  made. *(add-on for re-suspending)*
- A card saved from **another question** is left alone by this question's undo,
  and the dialog tells you so. A card saved from two questions stays saved for
  the other one.
- The **missed list** now names the questions you actually missed, not every
  question AnKing tags on a saved card. *(add-on)* Cards saved before 1.4 can't be
  told apart, and the popup says so.
- The **chapter** suggested first is the one matching the question's own system
  (read from your results page), then a subdeck you **already have** — reused
  even under another name for the same system (GI = Gastrointestinal, Pulm =
  Respiratory) — then the one you usually pick. Never the alphabetically first
  of a tie.
- **Picking a deck that is already a chapter** (`Missed Qs::GI`) saves straight
  into it — "Just GI" is the default — and no other chapter is suggested inside
  it. What *is* offered is an optional subdeck for the question's **subject**:
  Pharmacology or Pathology on Step 1, Medicine or Surgery on Step 2/3. The
  base deck is remembered as the root, **per Step**.
- **One suggestion per chapter:** First Aid's "Respiratory", B&B's "Pulm" and
  Bootcamp's "Pulmonology" — or "Medicine" and "Internal Medicine" — are one chip,
  not three.
- **Figures behind explanation buttons** (the blue pills in the explanation)
  can be attached when saving: Mnestic presses the site's own button, takes the
  image, and closes the site's viewer again.
- **Each matched card is readable at a glance** when choosing which to save:
  its whole Text with the answers in bold, what Extra and Additional Resources
  say, and how many images it has.
- **Empty fields are never shown** — a field holding only spaces, line breaks,
  empty boxes or an image with no source counts as empty.
- **Step 2:** if your subdecks are organ systems, system chapters (MSK,
  Rheumatology…) come before rotations (Internal Medicine…).
- Undoing a copy says how many **copies** it deleted (it counted their cards).
  *(add-on)*

## More accurate

- **The most specific card comes first** — in Preview, in Save, and in the
  image pages — so F shows the First Aid page for *this* question before the
  general ones.
- Image pages from several cards say which card each comes from, and images
  missing from your media folder are counted instead of silently skipped.
- **Weak areas** rank by accuracy adjusted for how many questions each topic
  has, so one unlucky question no longer outranks a real weak spot. Topics with
  fewer than 3 questions are marked "few questions" and listed last.
- Matches found through another Step's tags say so. The loose wildcard search,
  which can match COMLEX ids, only runs when you ask for a broader search.
- Coursology banks that aren't UWorld's (NBME, self-assessments…) are no longer
  matched against UWorld tags.

## Study tracker

- **Today, the streak and the heatmap no longer break when the clocks change.**
  A 10-day streak across Egypt's 29 October change read as 5.
- **Blocks counted by your qbank's dashboard are no longer booked to the day you
  happened to open it.** When the counter moved across several days, those
  questions count toward your totals but not toward one day's bar or streak.
- Rating an old question *Guessed* no longer counts it as answered today.
- **The finish date** uses your bank's own counter over your last 14 days, waits
  for 3 days of history before guessing, and shows the date at your daily target.

## Security and privacy

- **The add-on now allows only what Mnestic does.** Before, anything holding
  your pairing code could delete or overwrite any non-AnkiHub note. Now it can
  add only Mnestic's own tags, append only to Missed Questions, change only cards
  found by question id, in small batches, and delete only the copies Mnestic
  made. Oversized or malformed requests are refused before they're read.
  *(add-on)*
- Card previews **never load images from other websites**.
- Every button that talks to Anki — and every way of adding an image — needs a
  real click, paste or drop; a page's own scripts can't press them.
- The pairing code is hidden in the popup.

## For developers

`npm test` runs the unit tests, the add-on's guard and bridge-security suites and
the listing checks; `npm run test:e2e` drives the real extension. CI runs all of
them. Pure logic (dates, tracker, matching, tags, weak areas, cloze text) lives
in `extension/lib/`, each module unit-tested.
